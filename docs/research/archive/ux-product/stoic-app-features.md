Stoic App Features | Journaling, Mood Tracking & Daily Reflection Tools

# features.

More than a journaling app, stoic is your go-to resource for exploring stoicism and philosophy, mental well-being, happiness, productivity, relationships, and practical tips for living a fulfilled life.

## Journaling Inspiration and Guidance

### Daily Reflections

Begin and end your day with easily customizable reflections to keep your self-care practice intentional and fulfilling.

### Daily Prompts

Not sure what to journal about? Daily prompts are here to inspire you. Keep an eye out for a new prompt each day, and a new theme every week.

### Guided Journals

Explore a collection of thematic guided journals on topics that matter most. Revisit and explore them anytime.

## Helpful Tools for Building Your Habit

### Progress Tracking

Monitor your mood and mark the factors that influence it. Soon, you'll discover patterns and get insights to help you feel your best.

### Streaks & Badges

With fun rewards, staying motivated is easy! Build your streaks and unlock badges along the way. Can you collect them all?

### Gentle Notifications

Set smart reminders so you don't forget to log your mood or journal to stay consistent without disrupting your day.

## Write Your Own Journey

### Journaling History

Take a trip down memory lane whenever you want. Revisit your past entries to see how far you’ve come.

### Personalized Insights

Get valuable feedback and insights based on your daily entries and activities to better understand what influences your life.

### Treasured Memories

Go back to the most meaningful moments of your life and reflect on the memories that matter most.

## Mindful Exercises

### Meditation

Practice mindfulness through guided meditation sessions designed to center and calm your mind.

### Breathing

Improve your stress management and focus with simple, easy-to-follow guided breathing exercises.

### Writing Goals

Stay consistent with your journaling by setting personalized writing goals and celebrating your progress along the way.

## Words of Wisdom

### Quotes & Daily Affirmations

Get daily insights from the wisdom of notable thinkers, and practice gratitude by regularly reading and reflecting on affirmations.

### Collections & Resources

Explore a curated library of collections and resources for deeper learning on essential topics such as sleep, mental health, journaling, productivity, and more.

### Themed Events

Celebrate special occasions such as holidays and events with thoughtfully prepared journals, exercises, and lessons.

## There’s More!

### Privacy & Security

Rest assured, only you have access to your data. It’s securely stored on your devices and Apple iCloud, with every precaution taken to protect your personal writings.

### On All Your Devices

Stay connected and seamlessly switch your writings between your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and MacBook.

### Community

Join a supportive community where you can share your reflections, participate in discussions, and find encouragement.
