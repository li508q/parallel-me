## What is an AI Mental Health App?

An AI Mental Health App is a digital solution that uses AI technologies like Natural Language Processing (NLP), sentiment analysis, and generative models to offer real-time emotional support. These apps simulate human-like conversations, assess user mood, and provide personalized coping techniques or behavioral therapy suggestions.

Apps like Wysa, Replika, and Woebot use AI-powered conversations to help users manage stress, anxiety, and depression, without replacing traditional therapy but complementing it.

## How Does Wysa Work?

Wysa is an AI-driven therapy app that combines CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) tools, mindfulness techniques, and anonymized user data to create a non-judgmental, always-available mental health companion.

### Key Wysa Features:

AI chatbot for instant mood support

CBT-based journaling exercises

Mood tracking and analytics

Anonymous conversations with real therapists (paid tier)

Voice-enabled journaling
