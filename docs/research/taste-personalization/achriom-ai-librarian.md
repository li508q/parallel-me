## Questions

### How is this different from Goodreads or Letterboxd?

Those are great for tracking. Achriom is for understanding. We connect your books, movies, music, shows, and anime in one place, then use AI to find patterns and insights you'd never see in separate apps.

### What if I only have a small collection?

You can start with as few items as you want. The free tier includes unlimited items and 50 AI messages. After that, upgrade for unlimited.

### Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Start free — unlimited items and 50 AI messages included, no credit card required. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your library is yours. We don't sell your data, train models on it, or share it with anyone.
